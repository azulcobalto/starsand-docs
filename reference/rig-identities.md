# Rig identity and conversion reference

Rig prefixes are build codes and must not be treated as universal gender identities. The AnimationMap guide records remapping and F1 authoring; skirt and shoe studies preserve source/root and physical-fit limits. Supplied model filenames alone do not establish the root identity.

See [rig mapping](../systems/animation.md#a2-rigs-and-the-animationmap--the-core-mechanism), [skirt source audit](../../work/skirt-rig-feasibility/README.md), and [shoe fit contract](../../work/shoe-rig-conversion/physical-fit-contract.md).
